Who's Behind This

About

One person, building gaming PCs in Southern Oregon, and trying to do it honestly.

Hi, I'm Jacob Skrove. I build and test every PC here myself, and I started North Bridge PCs in May 2026.

It started with a PC I built for myself. I enjoyed the process, and realized I could build more and sell them. One build turned into a small business.

Right now, everything is local. I meet buyers in person, so you can see the system running before it's yours, and I skip the risks of shipping a complete PC. No middleman, no guessing who you're buying from. I may expand beyond local sales someday, but for now, local is where I'm focused.

PC building is one of my hobbies, not just a business. I'd rather match a system to what you actually need than push the priciest or flashiest parts. If a $500 build does the job, that's what I'll recommend. I want you to understand your PC, not just be sold on it.

What I can promise: I'll treat your build like it's my own, and I'll tell you exactly what you're getting, good and bad. I'm still new to this, and I'd rather say so than oversell my experience. I want North Bridge PCs to feel professional and trustworthy without pretending to be a big, established computer company.

I'm balancing this with school, so custom builds can take longer during the school year and usually move faster in summer. Either way, I'll give you a realistic timeframe, not a rushed guess.
