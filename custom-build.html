The Process

How a Custom Build Works

There's no complicated form. Reach out, tell me what you're working with, and we'll go from there, mostly over a few emails. The goal is a machine that fits what you actually need.

Send a Message →

01Tell me your budget

Give me a number you're comfortable with — a range is fine too. I'll work within it, not past it.

02Tell me what games you play

Different games pull on different hardware. Knowing what you play helps me prioritize where the budget goes.

03Tell me what features matter

Wi-Fi, extra storage, quiet operation, Linux (free for common distributions), RGB or no RGB. Anything that matters to you, say so.

04I'll recommend a parts list

I'll send over what I'm thinking and why. You can ask questions or suggest changes before anything is ordered.

05I build and test it

Once we agree on the parts, I assemble the build and run the same five steps every system gets.

06Pickup in person

We meet near Grants Pass and you can see the system running. Delivery may be possible for a fee.

No Tech Knowledge Required

Not Sure What Hardware You Need?

That's fine, most people aren't. You don't need to know which graphics card or CPU you want. That's the part I figure out.

What helps: your budget, the games you play, and anything specific you care about, like using more than one monitor, a quiet system, or Linux. If you don't know those either, say so. We can work backwards from "I want to play X and I have around $Y to spend."
