Who's Behind This

About

The short version: one person, building PCs in Southern Oregon, trying to do it honestly.

Hi, I'm Jacob Skrove. I started North Bridge PCs in May 2026.

It began the way a lot of these things do: I built a PC for myself, enjoyed the process, and realized I could build more and sell them. What started as one build turned into a small business.

Right now, everything is local. Selling in person means I can skip the complications and risks that come with shipping a complete PC, and it lets me deal directly with the people I'm selling to. No middleman, no guessing about who you're buying from. I may expand beyond local sales down the road, but for now, local is where I'm focused.

PC building is genuinely one of my hobbies, not just something I do for the business. I care more about finding good value and matching a system to what someone actually needs than about pushing the most expensive or flashy parts I can find. If a $500 build gets you exactly what you need, that's what I'll recommend, not something pricier just because it's pricier. I want you to understand what you're actually getting, not just be sold on it.

I want North Bridge PCs to feel professional and trustworthy without pretending to be something it's not: a big, established computer company. I'm still relatively new to this, and I'd rather be upfront about that than oversell my experience. What I can promise is that I'll treat your build like it's my own, and tell you the truth about what you're getting.

I'm also balancing this with school, so turnaround on custom builds and repairs can run a little longer during the school year. Summer tends to be the opposite. Less on my plate means I can usually turn things around faster. Either way, I'll always give you a realistic timeframe rather than a rushed guess.
